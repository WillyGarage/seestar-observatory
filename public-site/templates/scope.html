Needs dark sky (sun 12° down) and the scope in tilted mode. The scope moves by itself for a few minutes.
How to adjust Stand south of the scope, facing north (the way it leans). Left/right = turn the TH10's pan base. Up/down = the TH10's tilt knob: "lean more" lowers the dial number, "lean less" raises it. Make small turns, then hold still about 5 seconds so the stars can be re-read. Up/down follows the level sensor instantly. Under 0.5° is great, under 1° is fine. When both are green, press Measure again to confirm.
